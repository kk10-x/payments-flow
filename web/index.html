Follow a payment through the gateway.

Each particle is a payment passing through a gateway that routes it to one of three card providers. They end up in the ledger on the right, or they don't, and this page shows why.

Scroll ↓

Route

The gateway scores each provider by its recent success rate and latency, then sends each payment to a provider chosen at random, weighted by that score. A healthy provider gets most of the traffic. A brighter cluster means a healthier provider.

Degrade

Beacon is now declining 90% of charges. Its score drops, traffic drains toward Atlas and Crest, and after four failures in a row its circuit breaker opens. The cluster scatters and gets no traffic for three seconds, then one probe request tests it.

Retry

Atlas now charges the card, then loses 40% of its replies. The gateway can't tell whether that payment went through. So it retries Atlas with the same idempotency key, and Atlas returns the charge it already made. Sending it to Beacon instead could charge the card twice.

Failed declines, by contrast, charged nothing, so those do fail over.

Replay

A client that retries a request with the same idempotency key gets the original result, never a second charge. The pale particles bounce straight back from the gateway. Send one payment 25 times at once and count the charges.
